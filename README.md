# Bret Carpenter

**Managing Partner, Crag Co LLC | Systems Architecture | Engineering Governance**

I lead Crag Co LLC, an architecture and infrastructure solution design company focused on AI-enabled cyber-physical infrastructure, resilient communications, geospatial intelligence, and governed software systems.

My work focuses on the architectural and engineering disciplines needed to make complex systems more traceable, resilient, and governable.

## Areas of Focus

- **Systems Architecture:** Governed system design, state-transition architecture, and distributed infrastructure.
- **Software Engineering Governance:** Configuration management, change control, repository governance, and release management.
- **Evidence and Traceability:** Evidence provenance, technical records, decision traceability, and explicit verification status.
- **Cyber-Physical Infrastructure:** AI-enabled infrastructure, resilient communications, geospatial intelligence, and edge computing.

## Current Work

Developing Crag Co LLC's engineering governance framework, including repository-native documentation practices for managing changes, preserving evidence, and distinguishing proposed designs from approved requirements and verified implementations.

## Selected Repository

## Engineering Principles

I value architectural clarity, disciplined change management, traceable evidence, explicit authority, and the separation of design intent from demonstrated capability.

*Work in development is identified as such; implementation, verification, and approval status should be established independently.*
